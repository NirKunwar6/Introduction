ABOUT ME

Born in Achham, Nepal, I am a dedicated professional cricketer with a passion for excellence. I began my education at Shree Kinder Garden Academy in Sanfebagar before moving to Kathmandu to pursue my dreams at the highest level.

Based in Kathmandu, I now train at the prestigious Kathmandu Cricket Academy, where I continue to develop as an elite athlete. My journey has been defined by discipline, consistent hard work, and an unwavering commitment to representing my district and country with pride.

I come from a strong family foundation, with my parents Devraj Kunwar and Kaushilla Devi Kunwar being my greatest source of motivation. My brother Lokendra Kunwar has been instrumental in supporting my athletic pursuits.

As a professional cricketer, I believe in leading by example—through disciplined training, strategic play, and a mentality focused on winning. Every match is an opportunity to improve, and every tournament is a chance to contribute to my team's success and push myself to greater heights.

Core Values: Discipline • Excellence • Dedication • Teamwork • Integrity
